​Our Foundation: A Fight for Justice​

T​ he​​Mezzaria​​Flat​​Buyers​​Welfare​​Association​​was​​registered​​in​​2018,​​born​​from​​a​​collective​​will​
 ​to​ ​protect​ ​the​ ​rights​ ​of​​homebuyers​​who​​faced​​severe​​hardships.​​Our​​founding​​members,​​and​
  ​indeed​​many​​of​​you​​here​​today,​​invested​​not​​just​​your​​hard-earned​​money​​but​​also​​your​​dreams​
   ​into a home that was plagued by:​
               ​●​ ​Non-delivery of flats​
                ​●​ ​Severe construction delays​
                 ​●​ ​Deficiencies in promised amenities​
                  ​●​ ​A complete lack of accountability from the builders​
    ​Despite​ ​countless​ ​meetings​ ​and​ ​a​ ​string​ ​of​ ​unfulfilled​ ​promises,​ ​we​ ​united.​ ​We​ ​formed​ ​a​
     ​registered​ ​society​ ​to​ ​collectively​ ​pursue​ ​justice​ ​and​ ​to​ ​give​ ​our​ ​concerns​ ​a​ ​powerful,​ ​unified​
      ​voice before legal and administrative authorities.​
       ​Our​ ​perseverance​ ​paid​ ​off.​ ​After​​a​​delay​​of​​nearly​​three​​years,​​we​​secured​​the​​Occupancy​​and​
        ​Completion​ ​Certificates​ ​(OC/CC)​ ​for​ ​three​ ​of​ ​the​ ​six​ ​towers,​ ​finally​ ​allowing​ ​owners​ ​to​ ​take​
         ​possession​ ​of​ ​their​ ​homes.​ ​With​ ​this​ ​came​ ​access​​to​​the​​clubhouse,​​sporting​​and​​recreational​
          ​facilities, and our green spaces. This was a monumental victory, but it was just the beginning.​

​The Way Forward: Our Unfinished Agenda​

I​t​ ​has​ ​been​ ​over​ ​five​ ​years​ ​since​ ​the​ ​first​ ​residents​ ​moved​ ​into​ ​Mezzaria.​ ​Yet,​ ​the​ ​struggles​
 ​continue for many of our neighbors:​
       ​●​ ​Nearly​​half​​our community still awaits the registration of their flats.​
        ​●​ ​One-third​​of the towers lack the necessary OC/CC.​
         ​●​ ​Just under​​10%​​of our fellow buyers are yet to receive possession of their homes.​
          ​●​ ​Almost​​100%​​of us have complaints regarding facilities and services.​
  ​The​ ​time​ ​to​ ​amplify​ ​our​ ​voice​ ​is​ ​now.​ ​We​ ​have​ ​a​ ​formidable​ ​list​ ​of​ ​issues​ ​that​ ​demand​
   ​immediate attention and resolution. Our priorities for the coming year include:​

​A. Critical Completions & Legalities​
     ​1.​ ​OC / CC for Towers 2 and 3.​
      ​2.​ ​Registration of all unregistered flats.​

​B. Safety & Security​
     ​1.​ ​Comprehensive​ ​fire​ ​drills​ ​and​ ​validation​ ​of​ ​our​ ​fire-fighting​ ​plan,​ ​including​ ​on-site​
            ​testing.​
      ​2.​ ​Significant improvements in day-to-day maintenance and security services.​

​C. Amenities & Infrastructure​
     ​1.​ ​A fully functional clubhouse with air conditioning in all designated areas.​
      ​2.​ ​Upgrades to tower lobbies.​
       ​3.​ ​Upkeep of common areas and parks, including proper signage.​
        ​4.​ ​Securing full-capacity water and electricity connections.​
         ​5.​ ​Ensuring​​the​​full​​functionality​​of​​our​​Sewage​​Treatment​​Plant,​​gensets,​​water​​treatment​
              ​plant, and fire hydrants.​

​D. Quality & Compliance​
     ​1.​ ​Compliance with Green Building norms.​
      ​2.​ ​Essential repairs to tower facades and the final application of paint.​
