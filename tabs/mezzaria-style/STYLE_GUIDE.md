## 5. SALUTATION
- `Dear Madam,` / `Dear Mr. Jain,` / `Respected Sir,`
- Comma after name (not colon)

---

## 6. BODY TEXT
- Formal, legal-adjacent tone
- Numbered sections: `1.`, `2.`, `3.`
- Sub-items: `a.`, `b.`, `c.`
- Sub-sub-items: `i.`, `ii.`
- Indented paragraphs for sub-items (5-8 spaces)
- Line spacing: generous (1.5-2x)
- Paragraphs separated by blank lines
- Page numbers: `1/6`, `2/6` etc. at bottom-right

   Mobile: 8826055550                           Mobile: 9811428209

**Characteristics:**
- Title in bold, centered, large (18pt)
- Meeting details (date, time, venue) below title
- Attendees numbered with optional "(on phone)" notation
- Leave of Absence section for absent members
- Each point has bold number+title, then regular detail text
- "Points requiring clarity" section for ambiguous items
- Two-column signatory block at end
- Footer strip with registered office

```
                MINUTES OF MEETING

executives at the Facility Conference Room at 11:30 am on 4th August, 2025.

Participants on behalf of Quest for Safe Mezzaria:
   1. Arun Mehra                    919, Valencia
   2. Arun Sethi                    2311, Osimo
   ...

The Mezzaria Facility was represented by:
   1. Ashish Narula
   2. Pradeep Upadhyay
   ...

Following is the abstract of the discussions in the meeting.

    🔹 B1. Questions for the Facility Management – General

   ● B1-a: When will a certified Fire Officer be appointed? ...
       🔸 The Fire Officer will be appointed within August 2025. ...
```

**Characteristics:**
- Title in bold, centered
- Participants listed with flat numbers
- Q&A format with bullet points (🔹 for sections, ● for questions, 🔸 for answers)
- Flat numbers included for each participant
- Ends with signatory block

## 9. FONT & LAYOUT (from PDF analysis)
- **Body font:** Serif (likely Times New Roman or similar), 11-12pt
- **Headings:** Bold, same font family
- **Letterhead:** Larger, bold, with extra letter-spacing
- **Margins:** Standard 1-inch all around
- **Page numbers:** Bottom-right, format `N/total`
- **Line spacing:** 1.5x for body text

---

## 10. TONE & LANGUAGE
- Formal but not overly legalistic
- Uses "We humbly request" / "We respectfully submit"
- References specific laws: U.P. Apartment Act 2010, RERA 2016, NBC 2016
- References government orders by number
- Uses "Respected Sir" / "Dear Madam" for authorities
- Uses "Warm Regards" / "With gratitude" for closing
- Action items clearly marked as "Action Requested:"

---

## 11. DOCUMENT TYPES IDENTIFIED
1. **Formal Authority Letter** — to NA CEO, government officials
2. **Email-format Letter** — to developers, with email headers
3. **Meeting Minutes** — structured Q&A with participants
4. **AGM Speech/Narrative** — first-person, motivational
5. **Compliance Letter** — itemized list of missing documents
6. **Request/Appeal** — numbered sections with legal references
